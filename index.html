Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Web2Design and licensing.

How does the monthly free quota work?

Every user gets 5 full-page imports every calendar month at zero cost. When the new month begins, your quota resets back to 5 automatically. You can check your remaining credits directly in the plugin header.

How do I activate my Pro license?

After completing checkout on Lemon Squeezy, you will immediately receive your unique license key on the order confirmation screen and in your receipt email. In the Figma plugin, click the gear icon (or "Enter License Key"), paste your key, and click Activate.

Is my website data secure and private?

Yes, 100%. Web2Design processes all webpage structures and styles locally inside your browser and transfers them directly to Figma via the local clipboard. We do not store, log, or transmit any captured page content to remote servers.

Can I use Web2Design on password-protected or localhost sites?

Yes. Because the extension runs inside your active browser session, it can capture staging environments, localhost servers, and authenticated portals that external web crawlers cannot access.
